AQUATICMC RULEBOOK

Play fair. Make waves.

Our rules are here to keep AquaticMC competitive, enjoyable, and fair for everyone. The exact rules below are the rules that apply to the server and community.

⚖️
Know the rules before you play.

Not knowing a rule does not make it okay to break it. If you are unsure whether something is allowed, ask staff before doing it.

⚔️

Minecraft Server Rules

Gameplay, clients, exploits, and account activity.

  1. No Hacked ClientsDo not use clients that provide unfair advantages.
  2. No Movement ModsDo not use modifications that alter or automate movement.
  3. No FreecamDo not use free-camera tools to see or interact with areas you could not normally access.
  4. No ESPDo not use tools that reveal hidden players, entities, or information.
  5. No Auto or Easy PlaceDo not automate placement or use tools that make placement unfairly easier.
  6. No Macros or ScriptsDo not automate gameplay actions through macros or scripts.
  7. No Auto ClickerClicking must be performed normally by the player.
  8. No Mouse Tweaks/ ScrollersDo not use mouse features or scroll automation to gain an unfair advantage.
  9. No Crafting ModificationsDo not use modifications that change or automate crafting behavior.
  10. No Abusing BugsDo not intentionally use bugs or glitches to gain an advantage.
  11. No Attempted DuplicatingDo not attempt to create item or resource duplication methods.
  12. No Duplicating ItemsDo not duplicate items or knowingly use duplicated items.
  13. No Staff ImpersonationDo not pretend to be a member of the AquaticMC staff team.
  14. No Finding or Using the SeedDo not obtain, discover, or use the server seed.
  15. No Spamming Voice ChatDo not spam or intentionally disrupt voice communication.
  16. Report all Bugs, Glitches, and CheatersHelp keep the server fair by reporting problems and rule breakers to staff.
  17. Only 1 Alt Per PlayerEach player may have only one alternate account.
  18. No Account BoostingDo not use other accounts to unfairly progress, farm, or benefit an account.
💬

Chat Rules

Keep chat and the community welcoming.

  1. No Spamming or getting others to spamDo not flood chat or encourage other players to do so.
  2. No HarassingDo not repeatedly target, bully, or harass other players.
  3. No Advertising or PromotionDo not advertise or promote outside servers, services, or communities.
  4. No Discrimination or Hate SpeechDo not attack or target people based on protected or personal characteristics.
  5. No Death ThreatsThreats of violence or death are not allowed.
  6. No Sharing Others Private InformationDo not share another person's private or personal information.
  7. No Pretending to be Staff MembersDo not claim or imply that you are an AquaticMC staff member when you are not.
  8. No Ban EvasionDo not evade a punishment by using another account.
  9. Use common sense, so don't do things that will get you banned just because the specific rule isn't up hereIf something is clearly intended to bypass the rules or harm the community, do not do it simply because it is not listed word-for-word.
🛡️

When in doubt, ask first.

These rules are designed to make expectations clear. If you are unsure about a client, mod, exploit, account setup, or community interaction, contact the AquaticMC team before using it.
